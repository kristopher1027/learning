# Your challenge: Mini Calculator 🧮
Write a Python program that:
1. Creates two number variables: num1 and num2.
2. Calculates their sum.
3. Calculates their difference.
4. Calculates their product.
5. Calculates their quotient.
6. Calculates their remainder.
7. Calculates num1 raised to the power of num2.
8. Prints all the results using f-strings.
## Use these values:
num1 = 20 num2 = 6



## Expected output
===== MINI CALCULATOR =====
First number: 20
Second number: 6

Addition: 26
Subtraction: 14
Multiplication: 120
Division: 3.3333333333333335
Remainder: 2
Power: 64000000
==========================
